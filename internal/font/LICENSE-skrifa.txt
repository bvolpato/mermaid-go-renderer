0.46.2, read-fonts 0.43.3 and font-types 0.12.5 of the Fontations project
(https://github.com/googlefonts/fontations): autohint*.go and parts of
linux_scaler.go. Each file names its sources in its first comment. The crates
have the license "MIT OR Apache-2.0". The code is used under the MIT license,
